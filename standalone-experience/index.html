Something else on your mind?

Write to the Institute ↗
When can I join Acuity.One?

The connected membership is in development. Request an invitation to hear when it opens. Launch timing, pricing and final allowances will be confirmed before anyone is asked to subscribe.

What does one AI task include?

The proposed Free allowance counts a generated result: an Agent answer, a Search synthesis, a Codex output, a custom LumenNous practice or a myAeon reading. Reading the public Library and browsing the curated practice library do not use AI tasks. Final counting rules and media allowances will be published before launch.

What makes the experience personal?

You choose what to save in WisdomVault, and which saved items may inform a particular answer. That chosen context is intended to bring continuity to your study and practice without making every conversation part of your personal knowledge collection.

Will the Library and standalone tools remain available?

Yes. The Library remains an open reading room, free to read and download. The standalone tools remain separate options for people who prefer to use their own AI provider keys. Acuity.One brings the tools together as a managed membership.

Do I need to follow a particular tradition?

No. Acuity supports self-guided inquiry across traditions, with attention to the differences between them. You bring the questions and decide what has meaning for you. Generated interpretations should be read alongside their sources.

How should I use the personal and celestial reflections?

As invitations to reflection, held with your own judgement. Acuity supports study and contemplative practice. It does not provide clinical care, predict your future or replace a trusted teacher, counsellor or health professional.
