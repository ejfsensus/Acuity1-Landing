The inquiry

Acuity.Agent — a conversation with a library in the room

Ask about a teaching, a difficult passage or an idea that keeps returning. The Agent searches curated sacred texts and answers from the passages it retrieves, with references you can follow back to the edition.

  • Saved conversations you can return to and continue

Membership · WisdomVault

WisdomVault keeps what you choose, and nothing else

WisdomVault is not a silent record of every conversation. It is a private collection you curate: a passage that changed how you read, a reflection worth keeping, an insight from a chart reading.

  1. Choose

    Save a source, reflection or insight from any tool, one item at a time.

  2. Keep

    Review, rename or remove items whenever you like.

  3. Allow

    Mark eligible items to inform AI answers across the tools, only when you decide to.

Questions

Questions about Acuity.One

Can I join Acuity.One today?

Not yet. Both the Free tier and the Membership are coming soon. Requesting an invitation simply tells us you would like to hear when it opens; it does not create an account or a subscription.

What counts as an AI task?

An AI task is one generated result: an Agent answer, a cited Search answer, a Lens synthesis or Studio output in Codex, a custom LumenNous practice, or a myAeon reading. Reading the Library, browsing the curated LumenNous Library and using bookmarks do not use tasks.

How much will the Membership cost?

The subscription price has not been confirmed. We will publish it before Membership opens, and nobody will be charged without choosing to subscribe.

Does WisdomVault remember everything I write?

No. WisdomVault holds only the sources, reflections and insights you choose to save, and only items you mark as eligible can inform AI answers. You can review and remove them.

What happens to the standalone tools?

They remain separately hosted on acuity.institute and its tool sites, usable with your own AI provider key. Acuity.One is an additional, fully managed way to use them together.

Is Acuity a replacement for spiritual direction or therapy?

No. The tools help you research, study and reflect on texts and practices. They show their sources and their limits, and they are not a substitute for a teacher, counsellor or medical professional.
