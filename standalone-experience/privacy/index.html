Privacy policy

11 November 2026

Who is responsible

Acuity.Institute is the controller for personal data collected on this page, https://one.acuity.institute/. This policy covers this page only.

To ask about your data, or to use a right described below, write to data@acuity.institute.

What this page does

The page describes the forthcoming Acuity.One membership. It does not open an account, take a payment, or start a subscription. The form is a request to be written to when Acuity.One opens.

What you send with an invitation

If you submit the form, the page sends:

  • Your name, if you enter one. If you leave the name empty, the page sends the label “Acuity.One invite”.
  • Your email address.
  • The interest you choose: Not sure yet, Acuity.One Free, or Acuity.One Membership.
  • A short message the page writes for you. It says that you would like an invitation, records the interest you chose, and includes the address of the page you submitted from.
  • A submission identifier, the time of the request in UTC, and the fact that this is an enquiry rather than a request for project updates.
  • A marketing preference of “no”. This form does not add you to a mailing list.

The page does not ask for a birth chart, health information, or beliefs.

Where the request goes

The request is handled in the European Union.

  • Your browser sends it to this site. The site is hosted on Railway in the EU (Netherlands, europe-west4).
  • The site forwards the same request to the Acuity Institute enquiry workflow. That workflow runs on n8n, also hosted on Railway in the EU.
  • n8n checks the request, looks up the submission identifier, and, if that identifier is new, creates one record in a Notion database in the EU. Sending the same identifier again does not create a second record.
  • The workflow does not send an email. Acuity.Institute reads the Notion record and can write to the email address you gave.

The stored record contains your name, email address, the message, the time of the request, marketing consent set to no, a follow-up status of New, and the submission identifier. The workflow files the record with the Institute’s enquiry source, https://acuity.institute. The message itself states that it came from the Acuity.One invitation form and includes the address of this page.

Cloudflare publishes the domain and delivers the page. The invitation record is the Notion entry written by the EU workflow.

Why we use it

We use the invitation record to receive your request and to write to you when Acuity.One opens. That is a step you ask for before any membership exists. We use connection logs only to deliver the site, keep it available, and look into faults.

What we do not do

We do not sell this information. We do not use this form for advertising. We do not run an analytics or advertising tool on this page. The workflow does not send the request onwards by email.

How long we keep it

We keep the invitation record until we have written to you about the opening of Acuity.One, unless you ask us to delete it earlier or we no longer need it. Connection logs kept by the host are separate from that record and are kept only for operations and security.

Connection data

Serving the page creates technical data such as IP address, browser type, the time of the request, and the address requested. That data is processed by Cloudflare and by Railway so the page can be delivered and protected. It is not placed in the Notion invitation record.

Cookies

This page does not set its own cookies and does not use cookies to recognise you for marketing. The network that publishes the site may use a technical cookie to distinguish a browser from automated traffic.

Your rights

You can ask Acuity.Institute for access to the invitation record, for a correction, for erasure, for restriction, or for a copy you can take elsewhere where that right applies. You can object to use that is based on our operational interest in keeping the site available. Write to data@acuity.institute. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the same email address. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, including the authority in your country of residence. This policy does not name one, because no establishment address is published here.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. The current version is 11 November 2026.
