Guillaume Postel

04 / 19

Three offices, one scholar

  • Linguistic pioneer

    Arabic grammar and twelve alphabets, both in 1538. A Semitic family.

  • Christian Kabbalist

    First Latin Zohar on Genesis, and Sefer Yetzirah, in 1552.

  • Prophetic universalist

    Restitutio omnium, under one Pan-Sophia and a world monarchy.

ACUITY

Postel, 25 March 1510 to 6 September 1581. François I sent him to Istanbul in 1536 with Jean de la Forest. Chair at the Collège Royal, 1538. He joined the Jesuits under Ignatius Loyola in 1544 and was expelled. The Roman Inquisition held him in Castel Sant'Angelo, 1555–1559. From 1563 he was confined at Saint Martin des Champs, where he taught Scaliger and Raphelengius and aided the Antwerp Polyglot.

Venice, 1547

05 / 19

The New Eve, then the key

In Venice, Postel met Mère Jeanne, called the mater mundi and the New Eve, a feminine spiritual messiah with a perfect Kabbalah.

After her death he claimed her spirit immuted into his body, and he took the name Elias Pandocheus.

ACUITY

He was censoring Hebrew print for Daniel Bomberg. She was about fifty, and uneducated. The Clavis of 1547 is the Key to all things hidden in the constitution of the World. The Menorah treatise, Candelabri typici, followed in 1548, after the Hebrew broadsheet Or Nerot ha-Menorah. His Hebrew style-name was Ish Kefar Sekhanya u-Shemo Eliyyahu Kol-Maskalyah.

ACUITY

Franckenberg, friend and biographer of Jacob Böhme, republished the Clavis through Ioannem Jansonius, with a dedication to Werner of Pallant and an appendix, Key of the Key. Concord, he says, comes by the tenuous breath of grace, not by war or dogma. The chain he names: Joachim of Fiore, Pico della Mirandola, Reuchlin, Agrippa, Francesco Giorgio, Giordano Bruno, Julius Sperber. His phrase: Writing with Nature and Nature with Writing.

The Tarot

07 / 19

Seventy-eight, by the Name

Four suits of fourteen make the 56 Minor Arcana. Twenty-one numbered trumps and one unnumbered card make the 22 Major.

Commentators who cite Postel's Clavis and Lévi govern that pack by Yod-He-Vau-He.

ACUITY

The briefing calls this a mechanical apparatus of strict numerical laws. It attributes the system to esoteric analyses referencing the Clavis and Lévi's Dogme et Rituel, and Papus's Tarot of the Bohemians in the closing table. It does not say Postel designed the cards.

The ten, across three worlds

  • Divine world

    Emanation: 1, 2, 3, 4. The King, with aces, twos, and threes. The head.

  • Human world

    Evolution: 4, 5, 6, 7. The Queen, with fours, fives, and sixes. The chest.

  • Material world

    Evolution: 7, 8, 9, 10. The Knight, with sevens, eights, and nines. The body.

  • The transition

    The Knave holds the tens, and passes the suit on toward the Major Arcana.

ACUITY

The briefing links the worlds by repeating 4 and 7, and calls the ten the transition term. The material series includes 10, while the anatomy gives the tens to the Knave rather than the Knight. Both statements are kept.

The Rose Cross

11 / 19

Three epochs of the Rose

  1. 1610–1620

    Paracelsian reform

    Fama, Confessio, and the Chymical Wedding, Tübingen and Kassel.

  2. 1620–1660

    Stuart restoration

    Moray, Erskine, and Ashmole, in the networks for the Stuarts.

  3. 1710–1740

    High-grade revival

    Golden and Rosy Cross, Richter, Oetinger, then Herder and Hegel.

ACUITY

The briefing defines Rosicrucianism as an invisible, non-organizational Christian-Hermetic theosophy: spiritual knights keeping a feminine flame, Sophia or the Shekhinah, a rose outside and the blue fire of alchemy within. The Fama places C.R.C.'s learning in Damcar. The 1615 Frankfurt Confessio corrects that name to Damar, a city in Yemen. Precursors named here: Simon Studion's Naometria (1592) and Cruce Signata (1586); Dee's Monas, explicated by Gabella in 1615; Khunrath's Amphitheatrum.

Astro-spiritual

  • Angelic magic, of macrocosm and microcosm.

Pietist

  • Johann Valentin Andreae and Melchior Breler.
  • Tauler, Thomas à Kempis, and Johann Arndt.
  • Inward regeneration, and the devotional cross.

ACUITY

The briefing calls the right-hand camp Pietist and Lutheran, and traces it to German mysticism. Damar, in the Confessio, is also offered as a government of wise men and a model for European reform. The esoteric gloss of Damcar is blood of the Lamb.

Johannes Bureus

13 / 19

Monas, rune, Adulruna

  • Adulruna
  • Dee's Monas
  • Bjarkan ᛒ
  • Solar cross
  • Infinite circle

In 1610 Bureus set Dee's Monas on the rune Bjarkan. The Adulruna is a crucified sun in an infinite circle. He read the Northern runes as Skanzea's script.

ACUITY

Bureus, 1568–1652, archivist and tutor to Gustav II Adolf. A diagram header gives a death year before the birth. The prose and the closing table say 1568–1652, which this deck uses. The Japhetic line is from Postel's De Originibus: Noah, Japheth, Gomer, Askenaz, then Skanzea. The Monas was a 1564 copy from the Jesuit library at Posen. Manuals in 1611 and 1643. Pamphlets: Ara Foederis Therapici and FaMa e sCanzIa reDUX (1616), then Surge (1622). Bjarkan is paired with Aries.

Uppsala

14 / 19

Three crowns, three gods

  • Thor, the Father

    Lumen and divine law. Jupiter, and Thora as the law of the Jews.

  • Othin, the Son

    Verbum Dei and sapientia. Mars and Hercules, the eternal word.

  • Freya, Holy Spirit

    Divine goodness and fecundity. The Ephesian Diana.

ACUITY

Bureus claims Uppsala was founded before Abraham. The three golden crowns stand for three fylkelands, Tyunda, Ottunda, and Ferugra, and for these three gods. He calls Zamolxes and Abaris northern sages who taught Pythagoras, and he dates the sibyl Alruna to 432 BC.

Leo Septentrionalis

16 / 19

The Lion of the North

The prophecy

  • Paracelsus forecasts a northern chemical liberator.
  • Roeslin and Paul Grebner expand the forecast.
  • The name in German: der Löwe aus der Mitternacht.

The Swedish crown

  • Taken up by the crown and the Protestant League, 1630–1632.
  • Bureus names Gustav II Adolf the anointed liberator.
  • An Erfurt coin of 1633 shows him with sulphur and mercury.

ACUITY

Bureus says Gustav II Adolf will crush Apap, the papal antichrist and dragon, and open a seventh age of peace. The briefing's parenthetical dates for Helisaeus Roeslin are impossible and are left off this slide. Roeslin remains the author of Theoria Nova Meteoron (1578), on the 1572 Cassiopeia supernova, writing as Lampertus Floridus.

A reading

18 / 19

How to hold the briefing

Hold each system as its author's claim. The Tarot map cites Postel and Lévi. The 1615 Confessio corrects Damcar to Damar in Yemen.

ACUITY

Damar was a Sabean center in Yemen, linked in the briefing to the Queen of Sheba. The Ssabians of Harran, often conflated there with the Mandaeans, built planetary temples: triangle for Jupiter, square for the Sun, hexagon in a square for Mercury. Esoterically the briefing glosses Damcar as blood of the Lamb, a cover for the place-name Damar.
